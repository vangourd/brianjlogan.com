$ newsletter-encrypt

Encrypts an HTML article and an optional PDF attachment into an AES-256-GCM bundle (PBKDF2-SHA256, 250k iterations) using the BRZ2 format. Upload the bundle to /encrypted-newsletters/<slug>.bin; the share link opens the article reader at /newsletter/.

1. HTML article

2. Optional PDF attachment

3. Key

4. Slug

5. Encrypt

encrypted article ↓ download <slug>.bin

Upload to s3://brianjlogan/encrypted-newsletters/<slug>.bin via tools/upload-asset.sh <path> encrypted-newsletters/<slug>.bin --no-cache.
