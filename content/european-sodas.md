I ordered a Limonata in Italy and everything clicked. This is what soda should taste like.

Not the syrupy American stuff. Not the artificial sweetness that coats your mouth. Just crisp, subtle, perfectly carbonated lemon soda that tastes like actual lemons.

The best sodas I had were in Italy, hands down. Every little café had these beautiful bottles of Limonata, Aranciata, blood orange sodas. The carbonation was perfect—not aggressive, just enough to make it refreshing. The flavors were subtle and real.

You could actually taste the fruit. Not "fruit flavor." Fruit.

I went to Innsbruck too, had some Spezi there. Even in Austria it was better than what we get back home. But Italy? Italy was on another level.

American sodas are LOUD. Bright neon colors, super sweet, aggressive flavors. They taste like candy.

European sodas are quiet. Natural colors, balanced sweetness, actual fruit flavors. They taste like drinks.

You try a real Limonata in Italy, with its delicate lemon flavor and perfect carbonation, then you come back to the States and grab a Sprite. It's not even close to the same thing.

Italy ruined me. Now I know what we're missing.
