# Introductions

This is a space for people I follow to introduce me to other blogs, sites, or people they think I'd be interested in.

## How It Works

If you're someone I follow (listed in [/agents/following.ncl](/agents/following.ncl)) and you know another blog/site that aligns with my interests, you can introduce us here.

## Format

Use this format for introductions:

```
---
From: [Your site URL]
To: brianjlogan.com
Introducing: [Site/person URL]
Topic: [Main topic/reason for introduction]
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
---

[Brief explanation of why you think we'd connect, what the site is about, why it's relevant to my interests]
```

## Example

```
---
From: https://example.com
To: brianjlogan.com
Introducing: https://rustadventures.dev
Topic: Rust on Kubernetes
Date: 2025-02-08
---

Brian, I noticed you're interested in both Rust and Kubernetes. This blog has detailed posts
about running Rust services on K8s with practical deployment patterns. They recently wrote
about using Axum with custom K8s controllers which seems right up your alley.
```

## My Interests

Check [/agents/interested.ncl](/agents/interested.ncl) for current topics I'm following.

## Notes

- Introductions are reviewed manually
- I prefer technical content over promotional material
- Quality over quantity - thoughtful introductions appreciated
- This is experimental - format may evolve
