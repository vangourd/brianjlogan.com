I make soup every Sunday. Big pot, enough for the week.

Soup is the best food. Here's why.

# Why Nothing Else Comes Close

I throw a whole chicken in a pot with some vegetables and water. Six hours later I've got stock. Pick the meat off, add it back with noodles or rice or whatever's in the fridge. One chicken feeds me and my wife for three days.

Try doing that with a roast chicken. You get one dinner, maybe some sad leftovers.

Every cuisine figured this out independently. Pho in Vietnam, ramen in Japan, pozole in Mexico, borscht in Russia. Everyone landed on "put stuff in hot water and simmer it forever." There's a reason for that.

# Day Two is Better

Most food gets worse as leftovers. Soup gets better. The flavors sit there and think about what they've done and come back stronger.

I've never eaten fresh soup and thought "man I wish this sat in my fridge for 24 hours." But I've eaten day-old soup and realized it's better than it was yesterday.

# When You're Sick

Chicken noodle soup when you're sick isn't just nostalgia. It's liquid, it's protein, it's salt, it's easy to digest. It actually works.

Every time I get the flu, I can't recover without soup. Nothing else works. Just soup.

# One Pot, Done

I hate doing dishes. Soup is one pot. Maybe a cutting board. That's it.

Compare that to making a "real dinner" with a protein, two sides, different pans, timing everything. Soup doesn't care about timing. It just sits there getting better.

You can't overcook soup. You can definitely overcook everything else.

---

Soup is the most versatile, forgiving, economical food we've got. Hot day? Make gazpacho. Cold day? Make literally anything else.

I'm making pasta e fagioli this weekend.
