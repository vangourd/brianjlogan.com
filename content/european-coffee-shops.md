I didn't realize how spoiled Americans are with coffee access until I spent two weeks in Europe.

In America, you're one exit away from a 7brew, Dutch Bros, Starbucks, probably a local coffee shop with a drive-thru or a full sit-down place. Any town, any highway, there's coffee. I took that for granted.

Europe has cafes. Sit-down places where you order espresso at a bar or spend an hour at a table. The ritual is different. But finding a quick coffee to go? Way harder than I expected. No drive-thrus. No coffee shops every few blocks.

And the coffee they do have is mostly second wave French roast. Dark, bitter, over-roasted. The kind of coffee that tastes like burnt toast no matter where you go.

I missed the convenience. I didn't miss much else about American coffee culture, but I really missed just grabbing coffee without planning for it.
